Scope, privacy & evidentiary limitations

Rule-based educational triage only — this browser-only version does not use an AI/ML model and is not a certified forensic acquisition system. Hashes describe the bytes processed but do not prove origin or chain of custody. File signatures and PDF metadata are best-effort hints. This tool does not identify suspects, determine guilt, reconstruct events, or prove a crime occurred. Your browser, extensions, device, or downloaded files may independently retain information.
